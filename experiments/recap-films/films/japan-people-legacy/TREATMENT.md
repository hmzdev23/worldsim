# Japan earthquake timeline — treatment, draft 1

## Brief
A 30–45 second, vertical 1080×1920 event timeline, based only on WORLD//SIM's Japan earthquake fixture. Eight separate shots, rendered separately and assembled after review. Low-poly 3D; richly staged environments, varied materials and a broad natural palette. Human figures have human proportions and ordinary varied clothing, and appear at the correct scale in selected shots. Maintain the successful building-shake effect. No science-lesson segment or fictional-simulation label. Use the user's reference as a guide to legible, colorful environments and crowd density, not its exact designs, stage, people or text.

Style mixture: geographical context and camera flights from 3D Map Explainer; selective projected data/light over 3D buildings from Projection-Mapped Model; clear figures and visual hierarchy from Isometric Infographic in short inserts, without making the film a fixed-isometric diorama. The narrative remains an event timeline. Scene sets are illustrative interpretations of events, not measured reconstructions of specific streets.

## Candidate structures
1. **Chronological news timeline.** The earthquake, warning, evacuation, infrastructure failure, flood, aid, later aftershock, and recovery unfold in time order. This is the selected structure: it matches the user's explicit direction and allows each scene to show a distinct consequence.
2. **One person's journey.** Follow a resident through the same events. Rejected: it invents a personal story not present in the event data.
3. **Backwards reveal.** Start at recovery, then trace back through the crisis. Rejected: it breaks the requested forward timeline.

## Logline and arc
An earthquake off the Miyagi coast sets off a rapidly changing sequence of evacuations, outages, flooding, aid and recovery. Start at street level; broaden to the affected coast; end back on the ground as rail service returns.

## Reference
User-supplied low-poly audience/stage image: learn readable human scale, detail density, clear silhouettes, varied clothing and warm color. Do not reproduce the venue, text, characters, staging, watermark or exact palette. The rest of the camera plan is original.
