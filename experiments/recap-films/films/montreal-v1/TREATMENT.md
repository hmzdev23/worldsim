# Montreal pandemic — short-film storyboard

Based on the eight events generated by WORLD//SIM Director. A 40-second vertical timeline, without visible people, in low-poly Three.js. Warren narrates at a brisk pace, with legible short headlines, subtle music, and event sounds.

1. Day 1: A dangerous respiratory illness starts in Montreal; coughing spreads it. Low street view moves toward a hospital.
2. Day 2: Clinics expand testing as cases rise. Closer hospital view, testing sign and an ambulance.
3. Day 7: Local spread reaches Toronto. An animated regional map connects the two cities.
4. Day 14: New York transmission and hospital preparation. Map route traces the spread.
5. Day 20: Canada coordinates reporting, masking, ventilation, and hospital support. Public health headquarters.
6. Day 30: Absences disrupt travel, hospitality, and services. Quiet railway platform.
7. Day 60: Cases begin spreading locally in Paris. The map expands across the Atlantic.
8. Day 180: Infections decline and hospitals regain capacity; monitoring continues. A brighter city as traffic begins moving again.
