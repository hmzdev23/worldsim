# Montreal pandemic — revised shot plan

40 seconds, no visible people. Each event has a distinct setting, moving camera and action; narration remains brisk and grounded in the Director timeline.

1. Ambulance travels toward a hospital through Montreal with alternating emergency lights.
2. Camera tracks along a clinic testing machine as sample trays move through a flashing scanner.
3. Sweeping elevated view of Toronto with traffic and its tower on the skyline.
4. New York waterfront and bridge, with moving vehicles and a skyline at dusk.
5. Public-health operations room; screens and workstations light up as the response is coordinated.
6. Empty train platform with a delayed-service board; the camera tracks along the train.
7. New York-to-Paris route draws across a 3D map.
8. High-to-low tracking view over a city with traffic moving again.
