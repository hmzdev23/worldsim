# Assets and tools

- 3D city, suburban buildings, roads, cars and trains: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets) asset packs, CC0. The packs' `License.txt` files are included alongside the selected models.
- Animated [Man](https://poly.pizza/m/HMnuH5geEG) and [Woman Casual](https://poly.pizza/m/jpKRgGDxhk), by Quaternius via Poly Pizza, CC0. These remain in the Japan prototype for the earlier character experiments; the latest films hide people.
- Coast and boundary data: [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/), public domain. The simplified Japan and Montreal map data are included.
- The external renderer used for these samples is [Lemo-Opuscar](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar), pinned in the README. Its code, documentation and other assets have different licences; review its `LICENSE` before redistribution or commercial use. This handoff does not vendor Lemo's code or docs.
- Narration, sound effects and music: generated with ElevenLabs. Sample audio is included for team review and offline reproduction. The applicable ElevenLabs account and service terms govern its further use and distribution. API credentials are not included.
